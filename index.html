About

I am Saksorn (Harry) Ruangtanusak, an research scientist at SCB DataX in Bangkok, working with the Typhoon team on language models and agents.

My work spans LLM post-training, reasoning-model evaluation, tool-using agents, multilingual agent benchmarking, retrieval, and production AI systems. Previously, I worked at SCBX, CJ MORE, and AI and Robotics Ventures.

Through the SCBX–Stanford HAI Corporate Affiliate Program, I collaborated with Michael S. Bernstein and Jure Leskovec to develop AI research and use cases for the SCBX Group.

Currently, I am interested in how LLM agents can extend human capabilities while remaining reliable, controllable, and grounded in real-world workflows. I am especially interested in long-horizon human–agent collaboration and oversight.
