About

I am Saksorn (Harry) Ruangtanusak, an AI researcher at SCBX Group, working with the Typhoon team on language models and agents. I began my research journey in the US with nuclear reactor simulation (PHYSOR’22), advised by Yanin Sukjai and MIT NSE. I transitioned to AI in 2022, earned a gold medal in the National SuperAI Engineer Program, and held AI roles at CJ MORE and AI and Robotics Ventures.

Collaborating with Professor Michael Bernstein through Stanford HAI on agent simulation inspired my research focus on generative agent simulations. Since then, my research has covered agent evaluation (SEATauBench, EMNLP’26 Findings), role capability leakage (When a Kindergartener Solves Calculus, under review), tool-using agents (Talk Less, Call Right, EMNLP’25 WordPlay), and RAG (DoTA-RAG, SIGIR’25 LiveRAG).

I enjoy teaching and mentoring as a coach in the National SuperAI Engineer Program (2024–2025), and giving talks at universities and high schools. My students’ work includes He, She, It (NeurIPS’26 WiML), ThaiEOT (IEEE ICSEC’25), and SpeechConversationBench (arXiv).
