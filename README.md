I am an applied machine learning researcher at [SCB DataX](https://www.data-x.ai/) in Bangkok, where I work with the [Typhoon](https://opentyphoon.ai/) team on language models and agents.

My research spans LLM post-training, reasoning-model evaluation, tool-using agents, multilingual agent benchmarks, retrieval, and production AI systems. I am especially interested in long-horizon agents that can extend human capabilities while remaining useful, reliable, controllable, and grounded in real-world workflows.

## Selected work

- **[SEATauBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28715)** — adapting tool-agent-user evaluation to low-resource Southeast Asian languages. Accepted to Findings of EMNLP 2026.
- **[DoTA-RAG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.12571)** — Dynamic of Thought Aggregation for retrieval-augmented generation. Oral presentation at the SIGIR 2025 LiveRAG Challenge.
- **[Talk Less, Call Right](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.00482)** — improving role-play LLM agents through automatic prompt optimization and role prompting. Spotlight at the EMNLP 2025 WordPlay Workshop.
